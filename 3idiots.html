🎬 3 Idiots (2009)

⭐ 8.4/10

Director: Rajkumar Hirani

Genre: Comedy, Drama

Runtime: 2h 50m

📝 Full Review

3 Idiots is a heartwarming comedy-drama about three engineering students and their journey through college. The film stars Aamir Khan, R. Madhavan, and Sharman Joshi in lead roles.

The movie delivers a powerful message about following your passion instead of chasing success. It's funny, emotional, and inspiring all at once.

Aamir Khan's performance as Rancho is legendary, and the supporting cast is equally brilliant. The songs are memorable and the story stays with you long after watching.

Verdict: A must-watch for every student and every dreamer!
