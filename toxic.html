📝 Full Review

Toxic: A Fairy Tale for Grown-Ups is one of the most highly anticipated Indian action movies of 2026, starring Rocking Star Yash in the lead role. Directed by Geetu Mohandas, the movie brings together action, drama, crime, family emotions and a dark cinematic world.

Yash's screen presence is one of the major highlights of Toxic, while the supporting cast adds more depth to the film. Kiara Advani, Nayanthara, Huma Qureshi and Tara Sutaria are also part of this much-awaited project.

The movie combines high-energy action, suspense, and stylish visuals with a dark cinematic world. Although the rating is 5.1/10, the film is an entertaining choice for Yash fans and action movie lovers.

Verdict: A must-watch for Yash fans and South Indian action movie lovers!
