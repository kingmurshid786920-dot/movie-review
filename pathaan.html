🎬 Pathaan (2023)

⭐ 8.0/10

Director: Siddharth Anand

Genre: Action, Thriller

Runtime: 2h 26m

📝 Full Review

Pathaan is an action-packed Bollywood spy thriller starring Shah Rukh Khan, Deepika Padukone, and John Abraham. Directed by Siddharth Anand, the film combines high-energy action, suspense, comedy, and stylish visuals.

Shah Rukh Khan delivers a charismatic performance as Pathaan, while Deepika Padukone adds strong screen presence to the story. John Abraham is effective as the main antagonist. The movie features impressive action sequences, international locations, and entertaining moments.

The story follows Pathaan, an Indian spy who returns to stop a dangerous threat against the country. Although some parts of the plot are predictable, the fast pace and action keep the movie engaging.

Verdict: An entertaining choice for viewers who enjoy Bollywood action and spy movies.
