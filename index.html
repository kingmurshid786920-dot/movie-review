🎬 Pathaan (2023)

⭐ 8.0/10

Pathaan is an action-packed Bollywood spy thriller starring Shah Rukh Khan, Deepika Padukone, and John Abraham. Directed by Siddharth Anand, the film combines high-energy action, suspense, comedy, and stylish visuals. Shah Rukh Khan delivers a charismatic performance as Pathaan, while Deepika Padukone adds strong screen presence. John Abraham is effective as the main antagonist. The story follows Pathaan, an Indian spy who returns to stop a dangerous threat against the country. Overall, an entertaining choice for Bollywood action and spy movie fans.
