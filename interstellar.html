🎬 Interstellar (2014)

⭐ 8.6/10

Director: Christopher Nolan

Genre: Sci-Fi, Adventure, Drama

Runtime: 2h 49m

📝 Full Review

Interstellar is a visually stunning sci-fi epic about a team of astronauts who travel through a wormhole in search of a new home for humanity.

Matthew McConaughey delivers a powerful performance as Cooper, a former pilot who must leave his children behind to save mankind. The film explores themes of love, time, and sacrifice.

Hans Zimmer's score is breathtaking, and the visual effects are mind-blowing. The docking scene and the black hole sequence are unforgettable.

Verdict: A masterpiece that will make you think about space, time, and love!
